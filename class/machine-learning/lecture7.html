How to read this. Accuracy answers a question nobody asked: how often am I right, treating every mistake as equally bad? Drop % positive to and the always-negative baseline climbs to — a useless classifier now beats almost anything you can train, so the number stops carrying information.

Precision and recall split the two ways of being wrong: precision is about the flags you raised, recall about the cases that mattered. is their harmonic mean, so it is low unless both are decent.
